# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want FALCON to be a project anyone can take part in, whatever their
experience, background or identity. We pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- being welcoming to newcomers and patient with questions;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experience;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- showing empathy towards other people.

Things that are not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind;
- insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling;
- harassment, public or private;
- publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests and
discussions — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a
[private report](https://github.com/shriomverma100/FALCON/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting Team THETA172 directly. Reports are reviewed promptly and
handled in confidence.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
